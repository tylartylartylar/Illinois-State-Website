Welcome to Illinois


State Introduction

State order Statehood State climate

Illinois was the 21st state admitted to the Union. Located in the Midwestern region of the United States.

Illinois achieved statehood in 1818 and has since become one of the most populated states in the Midwest.

Illinois experiences a humid continental climate across much of the state, with hot summers and cold winters.

Illinois lies east of the Mississippi River and is bordered by Wisconsin, Iowa, Missouri, Kentucky, Indiana, and Michigan.

Illinois is known for its major cities, including Chicago, Springfield, and Peoria, as well as its agricultural regions and location along the Mississippi and Illinois Rivers.

The southern portion of the state generally experiences milder winters and a longer growing season than northern Illinois. Precipitation occurs throughout the year, with seasonal variations across the state.

Quick Facts

  • Illinois is ranks as the second flattest state in the United States, only beaten by Florida.

  • Abraham Lincoln was born in Kentucky before moving to Illinois, where he began his political career.

  • Illinois is associated with the development of the modern zipper, the Ferris wheel, and the first nuclear chain reaction, conducted at the University of Chicago in 1942.
