Springfield, Illinois


City Introduction

Springfield is the state capital of Illinois. Located about 1.5 hours drive north of St. Louis, Springfield sits in the south central region of Illinois and is about 1 hour drive south of Peoria, Illinois.

Springfield's downtown region is home to the Abraham Lincoln Presidential Library and Museum, which hosts a family friendly attraction showing a life history of Abraham Lincoln, including a full-scale log cabin inside the main entrance.

Population

2020 census population: 114,394

Incorporation:

Springfield was incorporated in 1832 and is the capital city of Illinois.

Region

Springfield is located in South Central Illinois, located south of Peoria, Illinois and north of St. Louis.

Classification:

Springfield is primarily a small urban city with semi-rural and rural areas nearby

Average Income:

Springfield’s average income is below the statewide Illinois average. Springfield’s median household income is $66,064, compared with the statewide median household income of $83,211. Springfield’s poverty rate is 16.8%.
